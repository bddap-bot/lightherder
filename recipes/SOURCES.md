# Where the reference frames come from

Every image in `ref/` is a frame of Dave Blair's own published Light Herder work,
taken from the platform poster frame the video is published with — Vimeo's oEmbed
`thumbnail_url` (CDN suffix `-d_1280` rewritten to `-d_2560`) or
`i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/maxresdefault.jpg`. No progressive MP4 was reachable: Vimeo's
player config endpoint answers 403 without a browser session. So these are real
frames the artist published, not seeks into a downloaded file, and no timestamp
applies to any of them. They are downscaled to 1600px on the long side.
